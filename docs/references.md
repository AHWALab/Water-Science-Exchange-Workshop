# Reading list for Module 1

## Core papers

- Zhang, J., K. Howard, C. Langston, B. Kaney, Y. Qi, L. Tang, H. Grams, Y. Wang, S. Cocks, S. Martinaitis, A. Arthur, K. Cooper, J. Brogden and D. Kitzmiller, 2016. Multi-Radar Multi-Sensor (MRMS) Quantitative Precipitation Estimation, Initial Operating Capabilities. Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society, 97, 621 to 638. https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-14-00174.1
- Zhang, J., L. Tang, S. Cocks, P. Zhang, A. Ryzhkov, K. Howard, C. Langston and B. Kaney, 2020. A Dual-Polarization Radar Synthetic QPE for Operations. Journal of Hydrometeorology, 21, 2507 to 2521. https://doi.org/10.1175/JHM-D-19-0194.1
- Kuligowski, R. J., 2002. A Self-Calibrating Real-Time GOES Rainfall Algorithm for Short-Term Rainfall Estimates. Journal of Hydrometeorology, 3, 112 to 130.
- Huffman, G. J. and coauthors, 2020. Integrated Multi-satellite Retrievals for the Global Precipitation Measurement (GPM) Mission (IMERG). In Satellite Precipitation Measurement, Springer, 343 to 353.
- Nelson, B. R., O. P. Prat, D. J. Seo and E. Habib, 2016. Assessment and Implications of NCEP Stage IV Quantitative Precipitation Estimates for Product Intercomparisons. Weather and Forecasting, 31, 371 to 394. https://doi.org/10.1175/WAF-D-14-00112.1

- Gourley, J. J. and coauthors, 2017. The FLASH Project, Improving the Tools for Flash Flood Monitoring and Prediction across the United States. Bulletin of the American Meteorological Society, 98, 361 to 372. https://doi.org/10.1175/BAMS-D-15-00247.1
